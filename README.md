# Tata Power – EV Charging Network Expansion Analytics

## Project Overview

This project focuses on evaluating EV charging infrastructure, customer charging behaviour, station utilization, and financial feasibility to support strategic expansion decisions for Tata Power's EV charging network in Bengaluru.

The objective is to analyse operational and financial datasets, identify demand patterns, assess station performance, evaluate investment opportunities, and determine high-potential districts for future EV charging infrastructure expansion.

An interactive Excel dashboard is developed to present key business metrics and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

- Analyse EV charging demand, energy consumption, and revenue trends.
- Evaluate charging station utilization and operational efficiency.
- Understand customer behaviour across income segments and EV models.
- Assess project profitability, investment costs, and return on investment (ROI).
- Identify high-potential districts for future charging station deployment.
- Evaluate infrastructure requirements against projected EV demand.

## Dashboard Modules

The dashboard is structured around four key analytical areas:

### 1. Business & Usage Overview
- Total charging sessions
- Total energy consumed (kWh)
- Total charging revenue
- Active customers
- Average revenue per session
- Average energy consumed per session

### 2. Station Utilization & Operational Efficiency
- Sessions per charging station
- Average station utilization
- Plugs versus actual usage
- Average energy consumption per station
- District-wise station distribution
- Sessions per plug

### 3. Customer & Demand Behaviour
- Unique customer count
- Sessions per customer
- Average energy consumption per customer
- Revenue per customer
- Charging behaviour by income segment
- Usage patterns across EV models and battery capacities

### 4. Financial Performance & Expansion Strategy
- Total capital expenditure (CAPEX)
- Annual revenue and EBITDA
- Project payback period
- Five-year ROI
- Subsidy impact on investment recovery
- Existing infrastructure versus projected EV demand
- District-wise expansion priorities

## Dataset Description

The project uses five interconnected datasets:

| Dataset | Description |
|---|---|
| `charging_sessions.csv` | EV charging transactions, energy consumption, timestamps and revenue |
| `charging_stations.csv` | Charging station locations, operators and infrastructure details |
| `customers.csv` | Customer profiles, income segments, EV models and battery capacities |
| `districts.csv` | District-level EV demand and infrastructure projections |
| `roi_bengaluru_districts.csv` | Project investment costs, revenue projections, profitability and ROI metrics |

### Dataset Statistics

- 114,000 charging session records
- 8,000 registered customers
- 65 operational charging stations
- 18 Bengaluru districts
- 98 proposed EV charging projects
- 5 interconnected datasets

A separate data dictionary is included to document dataset attributes, data types, primary keys, relationships and field descriptions.

## Tools & Technologies:

- **Microsoft Excel:** PivotTables, Pivot Charts, Excel Formulas, KPI Calculations, Interactive Dashboard, Data Analysis
- **CSV Datasets:** Structured data for operational, customer and financial analysis.

## Expected Business Outcomes

The analysis aims to provide insights into:

- Charging demand and customer usage patterns.
- Operational efficiency across existing charging stations.
- Customer segments contributing to charging demand and revenue.
- Financial viability of proposed charging infrastructure.
- Districts offering stronger investment and expansion potential.

These insights can support infrastructure planning, investment prioritization and strategic decision-making for EV charging network expansion.
